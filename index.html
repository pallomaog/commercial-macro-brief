Data and method

Company data. Harbor Ledger is fictional: a U.S. software vendor selling customs compliance and landed-cost tools to importers. Deals (Jan 2023 – Oct 2026; 2023 is used only to set 2024 targets) are generated in the page with a fixed seed. It reproduces the structure of a real B2B dashboard without using any employer data.

Macro data. FRED, retrieved 24 Nov 2026: federal customs duties (B235RC1Q027SBEA, $bn, SAAR, quarterly, BEA); import price index, all commodities (IR, BLS, monthly; Oct 2025 not published); federal funds effective rate (FEDFUNDS, monthly); real broad effective exchange rate for the U.S. (RBUSBIS, BIS, monthly).

Definitions. Win rate = won ÷ (won + lost). Coverage = open deals whose expected close date falls in the next quarter ÷ next quarter's target. Targets are set at 110% of the prior year's bookings for the same quarter.
